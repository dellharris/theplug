Why tokens

Why tokens and not just cash.

01

It is a game.

Earn, stack, spend, repeat. Watching your tokens grow makes creating more fun and keeps people coming back.

02

Whole numbers, not cents.

Each token is worth less than a dollar, so rewards show up as clear whole numbers you can track instead of fractions of a cent.

03

It stays in the family.

Tokens earned by creators get spent on other creators' campaigns, so the value keeps moving between us instead of leaving.

04

Performance first.

Tokens come from posts that actually performed, and cash out unlocks after a threshold, so rewards go to people who deliver.

Built for trust

How we protect everyone's money.

  • Real people review creators before they join. Bots do not get in.
  • Payouts follow measured performance, not follower count.
  • Campaign budgets are capped. Spend stops when the budget is gone.
  • Unspent tokens go back to the campaign owner.
  • Cash out unlocks after a threshold of earned tokens.
  • Campaign owners pick who receives products, using past performance.

The basics
What is The Plug?

DecidedThe Plug is a creator to creator network from Idu House. Artists, brands and event hosts launch a campaign for a song, product or event. Creators make their own content for it and earn based on how far their posts travel.

Who can use it?

DecidedIdu House members launch campaigns, and members get first priority. Content creators can join the network and make content without being a member. Everyone is reviewed by real people before getting in.

Is it live?

Not yet. Right now there is a working prototype with sample data, so you can see how it flows. Info sessions run about every two weeks until the network hits critical mass, then the beta opens. A working demo is targeted in about a month.

Tokens and money
How do tokens work?

DecidedCampaign owners buy tokens and put a budget behind a campaign. Creators earn tokens when their posts perform. You can spend tokens on your own campaign or cash out once you pass the threshold. You can do both: launch your own campaigns and create for others.

What is a token worth?

Being finalizedTokens are bought with real money and each token matches a dollar value under one dollar. The exact rate will be published before beta, and it will be set so the numbers stay sensible and easy to track.

How do I get paid?

DecidedTokens are earned from the performance of your post, like views and reach. The campaign's budget only goes down as creators actually earn.

When can I cash out?

Being finalizedThere is a minimum number of earned tokens before you can cash out. It will be reasonable. It exists so tokens keep circulating between creators and so payouts go to people who performed. The exact threshold is published before beta.

What if a campaign does not perform?

DecidedCampaign owners set a budget and a timeline. If the metrics do not add up by the end, the unspent tokens go back to the owner. Creators keep whatever tokens they earned, so nobody's time is wasted.

Trust and quality
How do you stop bots and scams?

DecidedThis is a quality network, not a scale network. Real people review creators before they join, and rewards come from measured post performance, so a pile of fake followers does not help. We also keep a performance history so campaign owners can see who delivers.

Being finalizedThe exact verification steps and anti-fraud checks are being built and tested before beta.

Do I need a lot of followers?

DecidedNo. Earnings follow the quality and reach of your content, not the size of your page. A small account with a post that travels can earn more than a big account with a post that sits.

Can anyone cash out right away?

DecidedNo. Tokens come from posts that performed, and cash out unlocks after a threshold. That protects campaign budgets and rewards real results.

Campaigns
What can I promote?

DecidedSongs, products and events, online or in person. Products start with digital and IP products.

Being finalizedSending physical samples is planned for later. Campaign owners will hand pick who receives them, using each creator's performance history.

Is this instead of running ads?

DecidedNo, it works alongside ads. Ads grow your own audience. The Plug puts more people making content about your release, which helps the algorithm see it. If a creator makes something great, you can ask permission to use it as ad creative.

Can I bring other creators in?

Being finalizedYes. A partnership program is planned that rewards members who bring creators into the network. The details come before beta.

What is next
When does the beta open?

DecidedAfter the network reaches critical mass. Idu House members get first access, and creators get first access to the creator network. There will also be an in person version tied to our tech convention.

Use the quick survey. Feature requests and questions are read and followed up on, and they shape what gets built.
