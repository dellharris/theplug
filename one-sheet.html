Creator to Creator Promotion Network

Launch a campaign for your song, product or event. Creators post. The better a post performs, the more of your budget it earns, until the budget is gone.

Your follower count does not matter. Post reach is the only thing that counts. A creator with 800 followers whose video travels earns more than a creator with 800,000 whose video sits. Micro to macro, everyone starts on the same line, and everything is paid in Plug tokens.

Launch a Campaign

  • Pick a song, product or event
  • Buy tokens to fund the budget
  • Creators make content for you
  • Budget is released as posts perform
  • Campaign ends when the budget is gone

Create and Earn

  • Join any open campaign
  • Upload your post to the campaign
  • Earn tokens based on post reach
  • Repost and share other campaigns to earn more
  • Better content earns a bigger share

Spend or Cash Out

  • Put tokens into your own campaign
  • Or cash out once you hit the threshold
  • Create amazing content, get paid
  • Every creator is also a promoter
